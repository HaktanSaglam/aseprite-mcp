# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be welcoming, be patient with beginners, give and take feedback gracefully, and never
harass, insult or demean anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere
you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer,
[@HaktanSaglam](https://github.com/HaktanSaglam), privately. Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that break this code, and
may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously do so, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
